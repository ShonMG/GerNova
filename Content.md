# 18. SEO PRIORITIES FOR LAUNCH

The website should prioritize these areas:

**1. Technical SEO**
Fast loading, responsive design, clean URLs, crawlability, sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs and HTTPS.

**2. Search Intent**
Each service gets its own dedicated page rather than placing everything on one page.

**3. Helpful Content**
Publish genuinely useful articles answering questions potential customers are searching for.

**4. Local SEO**
Where applicable, optimize for relevant Kenya-based searches and maintain an accurate Google Business Profile.

**5. Structured Data**
Implement appropriate Organization, WebSite, Service, Breadcrumb and FAQ structured data where eligible.

**6. Internal Linking**
Connect blog articles, service pages, pricing, case studies and contact pages.

**7. Conversion Optimization**
Every important page should have a clear next action.

**8. Performance**
Optimize images, JavaScript, CSS, fonts and hosting to provide a fast user experience.

**9. Authority**
Build genuine backlinks through partnerships, business directories, industry publications, guest contributions and useful resources.

**10. Measurement**
Connect Google Search Console and analytics so GerNova can measure impressions, clicks, rankings, traffic, enquiries and conversions.
